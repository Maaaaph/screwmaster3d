About Screw Master 3D

Screw Master 3D: Pin Puzzle is an addictive puzzle game that challenges your spatial thinking and problem-solving skills. In this engaging 3D puzzle experience, you'll need to manipulate various objects by screwing and unscrewing pins to solve increasingly complex challenges.

Each level presents a unique puzzle configuration where you must figure out the correct sequence of moves to complete the objective. The intuitive controls make it easy to pick up and play, but mastering the later levels will require careful planning and strategic thinking.

With stunning 3D graphics, satisfying mechanics, and a progression system that keeps you coming back for more, Screw Master 3D offers hours of brain-teasing entertainment for puzzle enthusiasts of all ages.

How to Play

1. Twist & Turn

Use simple touch or mouse controls to rotate screws and pins. Drag to turn objects and solve the mechanical puzzles.

2. Solve Puzzles

Each level has a unique objective. Figure out the correct sequence of moves to complete the puzzle and advance to the next challenge.

3. Progress & Master

Start with simple puzzles and work your way up to mind-bending challenges that will test your spatial reasoning and problem-solving abilities.
